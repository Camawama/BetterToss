## [Unreleased] (1.0.2)

### Added
- Projectiles that hold their own speed are charge-thrown too (2026-11-21; reported: a throwing disc could not be charged with Better Toss). Two things stood in the way. The disc is a sword-like item (one to a stack, with durability) whose right-click throws a thrown-item projectile at once; the start-up scan's second pass left it out as "a thing that shoots, not a thing thrown" (only the entity type's default item, the iron disc, was found by the first pass, and the others were learned one by one after a first, uncharged throw). And the disc entity sets its velocity back to its tier's speed on every tick, flies level for a set number of ticks and comes back to the hand, so even a charged launch was the item's own one tick later.
  - The scan now takes a non-stacking item with durability when the projectile its own right-click code makes is a thrown-item projectile and that code takes the item from the stack: the item itself is what flies and leaves the hand, so it is a thing thrown (a disc, knife or axe that comes back), not a thing that shoots. A tool that casts a thrown-item projectile on a line and keeps it in the hand (Spelunkery's pick on a stick) takes nothing from the stack and is still left alone. All five discs are throwable before any throw.
  - A projectile that keeps its own speed is told on its first tick (it was given one speed and holds another) and, when the charge asked for more than its own speed, is carried that much further along its heading after each tick of its own (`ProjectileDrives`; server config `driveFixedSpeedProjectiles`, on), so a charge takes it the charged distance in the flight time its mod gives it, through its own bounces, hits and return; the push stops short of a block or an entity in the extra stretch and leaves them to the projectile's own next step, so its mod's hit and bounce code runs as before. Each projectile is judged and carried only as its own level ticks, whatever other worlds are loaded, and only on a tick in which it moved itself (a `/tick freeze` or `/tick step` holds it as it holds the projectile). Clients tracking it are told the push (a new clientbound message; the network protocol is 4) and move their copy the same way. A quick click leaves such a projectile at its own speed: it can't be thrown slower than it flies.

### Changed
- A full bar throws a little less far: `maxPowerMultiplier` 1.6 -> 1.5 (a lob's range goes with the square of its speed, so about an eighth less range at a full charge; the empty bar and the Q fling are unchanged). The server config now carries a `configVersion`, and a world's file still holding the old default is moved to the new one as it loads (a value somebody changed is left alone), as the client config has done since 1.0.0.

## [Unreleased] (1.0.1)

### Added
- Thrown items that no entity type names are charge-thrown from the first throw (2026-11-14; reported: the rocks of Spelunker's Charm, Spelunkery's pebbles and its glowsticks were not). Three kinds of item got past the start-up scan, which asks every thrown-item projectile type which item it shows: a block item that is thrown instead of placed (the glowsticks and Mineomite: block items were left out as blocks), many items sharing one projectile type (seven pebbles, seventeen glowsticks: a type shows one of them), and a projectile that is an arrow underneath (the rocks, as every MCreator "ranged item": not a thrown-item projectile at all). The scan now has a second pass (`server.UseAnalysis`, server config `scanItemUses`, on): it reads the right-click code of every modded item's class, the class files as data with nothing loaded or run, and follows it through the mod's own methods and lambdas (six calls deep, 400 methods at most) to see whether it makes a projectile. Such an item is taken when it is a thing thrown and not a thing that shoots: it stacks, has no durability, is not food and is not held or drawn. A projectile that is a firework rocket is a boost, not a throw (Alex's Mobs' enderiophage rocket). The log line of the scan lists what was found and what was not taken and why; the `not_throwable` tag and `throwableBlacklist` still win.
  - In the main pack the second pass adds the four rocks of Spelunker's Charm, Spelunkery's seven pebbles, seventeen glowsticks and Mineomite, CoFH's grenades, the Aether's lightning knife, Alex's Mobs' emu and other animal eggs, Biomancy's acidic egg, Vanilla Tweaks' dynamite, Supplementaries' cannonball, Galosphere's flares and bombs, the Undergarden's blisterbomb and Twilight Forest's ice bomb and Spelunkery's eggplant where the first pass had not found them already. Left alone on purpose: wands, fishing rods, the potato cannon, crossbows and other things with durability or a stack of one; Caverns and Chasms' bejeweled pearl and Sully's throwing knife (drawn, then thrown on release: their own charge); Alex's Mobs' pocket sand (its shot is not a projectile, so there is nothing to give a speed to). Galosphere's glow and spectre flares are firework rockets underneath and stay as they are.
  - **Measured** (pack harness `toss_uses` with `toss_iceball`, the main pack): the second pass read 1347 item classes in 0.4 s together with the first pass, 51 make a projectile, and it found 44 items more; a rock, a stone pebble and a red glowstick each left a 32-tick hold 4.33 times as fast as a click (the bar's power 0.93: expected 4.33); a glowstick clicked at the ground was placed and not thrown; the 17 items that must not be taken were not.
  - A thrown block item is still placed when the click is aimed at a block, as its mod has it; aimed at nothing it is charged.
  - The learner takes any projectile that shows the item used, not only thrown-item projectiles (an item of the game's own classes only with a thrown-item projectile, so a firework boost teaches nothing).
- Other mods' hand-thrown items are charge-thrown from the very first throw (2026-10-31; reported: hand-thrown items kept turning up that had to be allow-listed, lately the IceCaves backport's `ice_caves:ice_ball`). The IceCaves backport's ice ball is a plain `Item` whose use throws a `ThrowableItemProjectile` showing the ice ball (1.5 blocks a tick, a snowball's speed), so the class test missed it and the learner only knew it after its first, uncharged throw. Now a start-up scan (`server.ThrowableScan`, server config `scanThrowableProjectiles`, on) runs at every world start before anyone throws: each misc-category entity type is created once in the overworld (never spawned, discarded at once) and, when it is a thrown-item projectile, the item it shows is a candidate; the candidate is charge-thrown from then on when it is plainly an item thrown by hand (not a block, not food, nothing held or drawn, and a class of its own overriding `use(Level, Player, InteractionHand)`, matched by parameter and return types since production runs on SRG names), so a mob's spit whose projectile shows some other item stays out. The finds join the known set and are synced to clients with the learned ones, but aren't saved: the scan runs again each start. The `not_throwable` tag and `throwableBlacklist` still win; `Throwables.knownByItself` is the tag/list/class test alone, `Throwables.learnAll` adds the scan's finds. Checked in the main pack (which has IceCaves): the scan looked at 522 misc entity types, 51 of them thrown-item projectiles, and found 14 hand-thrown items (the ice ball, Galosphere's palladium bomb, Biomancy's toxin grenade and acidic egg, Undergarden's blisterbomb, Spelunkery's stone pebble and eggplant, Vanilla Tweaks' dynamite, the Aether's lightning knife, Alex's Mobs' cockroach ootheca and emu egg, Farmer's Delight's rotten tomato, Quark's parrot egg, Supplementaries' bomb), 17 known already (vanilla's, Enderman Overhaul's pearls, Cardiac's bottle), 20 rejected (mob spits and shards with no use of their own, foods, blocks, a drawn pearl), one type failing to construct, 510 ms; `toss_cardiac` still passes on this jar (its learned round empties the known set and reloads the tag, so the scan's finds don't stand in). Pack harness `toss_iceball` (the main pack): the scan's finds name the ice ball and it is throwable before any throw, and from the real use key the very first click must leave at the empty bar's power (1.5 x 0.35), a 32-tick hold at the bar's value, a second click at the empty bar's again (the three throws still to be verified).

### Fixed
- Holding Q to fling the selected item no longer drops a second item on the release (2026-10-31; reported: releasing a held Q sometimes threw the charged item and dropped a second one as well). Cause: holding a key makes the system repeat it, and each repeat is a click in vanilla's counter; Better Toss sweeps those clicks at the start of every client tick and ignores them while a charge is on, but a repeat that arrived in the same input batch as the release was still uncounted when the release threw, so the next tick's sweep read it as a fresh tap of a key that was already up and dropped an item (as often as a repeat happened to land in the release's frame). Fix: when a charge ends (released or cancelled) the clicks still counted on its key are drained (the drop key for a Q fling, the use key for an item or caught throw: mouse buttons never repeat, a key bound to use could), a drop-key click read in the same client tick a drop charge ended is ignored, and a tap held long enough to repeat (a long `dropHoldTicks`) drains its repeats too. Quick taps are untouched.

- Cardiac's life bottles are charge-thrown like XP bottles (2026-10-29; reported: life bottles couldn't be charge-thrown). The bottle is a plain item whose use throws a projectile of its own, so the class test (anything extending a vanilla throwable item) never saw it; it is now an optional entry in the `bettertoss:throwable` tag.
- Other mods' throwables built the same way are learned from their first throw: when an item's own use throws, at once and without a draw, a projectile showing that same item (the way vanilla throws a snowball or an XP bottle), it is charge-thrown from then on, saved with the world and synced to every client (server config `learnThrowables`, on). Its first throw in a world is the item's own. The `bettertoss:not_throwable` tag and `throwableBlacklist` still win. The network protocol is 3 (a client and server must both have this version).

### Changed
- Tridents are vanilla's again by default: their own draw, throw, Loyalty and Riptide, no charge bar, whatever the tags and lists say. Server config `tridents` (false) takes them over as before: the charge bar, its power, focus and strain apply to a trident throw.

## [1.0.0]

### Added
- Hold-to-charge throws for every throwable item, with an oscillating (or ramping) power bar in the XP bar's slot. A quick click still throws straight away as a weak, uncharged toss (empty-bar power, widest spread), with no throw cooldown, so eggs and snowballs can be spammed; the hold that starts a charge is configurable (`useHoldTicks`, 5 ticks). Tridents charge from the first tick.
- Flick throws: mouse motion at the instant of release bends the throw, with configurable thresholds, strength and caps.
- Focus: quick clicks and early releases have a wide spread that steadies over the first two seconds of the hold, with a slight FOV zoom. Optional one-line tips, off by default.
- Strain: a charge held too long gets shaky, the way a spear does when held out too long. The hand is steady at first, then trembles more and more (from 2 s, fully by 5 s: server `strain.steadyTicks` 40 and `strain.shakyTicks` 100), in first person and on the third-person arm, and the throw scatters by up to 4° more (`strain.maxInaccuracyDegrees`). How much the hand trembles is the client's `strain.handTremble` (1, 0 turns it off). This replaces the old camera and hand shake, which ran the other way (shaky at the start, steadying as you held) and was off by default: `cameraShake`, `cameraShakeDegrees` and `handShake` are gone, and `barShake` now shivers the bar with the strain instead. The tremble, the charge bar and the spread are Camafy's shared hold-to-charge mechanic, the same as Nice Catch's rod casts (needs Camafy 0.1.13).
- Client config migration: settings still on an old default (tips and shakes on, from the first builds) move to the new default; settings you changed are kept.
- First-person wind-up and whip animations, and third-person wind-up, throw and holding poses visible to other players.
- Drop-key flings: tap Q to drop, hold Q to charge a throw of the selected item (Ctrl for the whole stack). Bigger stacks are heavier and fly shorter, with the reachable part of the bar shown.
- Catching: hold the catch key (right-click by default) to catch incoming projectiles; caught projectiles stay projectiles, must be thrown before anything else can be done, and drop in place on logout.
- Generic compatibility: any item that spawns a projectile from its use() is charged, with item/entity tags and config lists for the rest.
- Extensive server and client configuration.
